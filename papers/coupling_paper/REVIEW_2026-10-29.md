# Internal review of the merged paper (29 Oct 2026)

Two read-only reviews of `manuscript/coupling_paper.tex`: a referee-style review (recommendation: major revision,
writing and re-analysis only) and a copy-edit / consistency audit. What was changed the same day, and what is open.

## Done (29 Oct)
- Framing: the single path is presented as the consequence of the minimal, equation-preserving coupling, not as a
  discovery; "first coupling" replaced by the difference to multi-species continuum models (Alpkvist & Klapper 2007,
  Rahman et al. 2015, new citations); Rodriguez et al. 1994 and Seminara et al. 2012 cited.
- Antibiotic effect: "no effect on the converged seed stress is resolved beyond the grid uncertainty" (6.55 vs 6.42e-4 Pa,
  GCI 0.7 and 1.4 %); only extrapolated values compared; growth "2 % or less" (Table tab:gci: 9.16/9.19, 8.56/8.71).
- Mixed seed: "about half" replaced by the extrapolated ratio 0.7 with its 29 % band.
- Domain level: at the full front speed the cube is filled in every example (colonised fraction 1, checked in the JSONs);
  at r/10 the antibiotic enlarges the colonised volume (0.27 vs 0.23), stated.
- Inherit rule: new points start alive, so a dead front still produces living material; this drives the maze recovery;
  stated in Sections 2.3, 4.4 and the conclusions.
- Time link: the antibiotic onset 1/3 is a fraction of the run, not a duration in the point model's own time.
- New Discussion paragraph "Antibiotic tolerance" (Walters et al. 2003, Stewart & Franklin 2008): the point model kills
  fastest at low nutrient, experiments often show starved regions most tolerant.
- Verification wording: MMS in the finite-volume and Galerkin codes; the ANSYS element agrees within its band (title no
  longer says "in three codes").
- Molar: "A molar crown geometry", a = c in the film explained, limits (two-cell ring, rigid crown) stated.
- Numbers: eight (not ten) times faster (21 vs 2.7 per T*), 13 times (16 vs 1.2), 1.6 to 3.3, outer steps 0.025 to
  0.0025; notation E_0, phi_3D in the mechanical term, species A = i = 1; Pe/Co upright, D defined; voice ("we") removed.
- Abstract 246 words (BMMB 150-250), no citations; keywords 6.
- Comparison with experimental scales shortened.
