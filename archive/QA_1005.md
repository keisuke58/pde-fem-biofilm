## A. Scope and contribution

**1. What exactly is your contribution in the FEM part?**
EN: Two things. A growth law F = Fe Fg, Fg = αI, as an ANSYS user material,
verified against closed forms and coupled to the element's biofilm field at every
Gauss point. And for two species, the point model of Klempt et al. 2026 at every
Gauss point: the field sets the amount, the point model the composition.
JP: 2つ。成長則のUSERMAT（閉形式で検証、Gauss点で場と結合）と、2種の点モデルを全Gauss点で回すこと（量は場、組成は点モデル）。

**2. Why only one and two species?**
EN: For one and two species the papers give numbers I can check against. The
five-species model is calibrated (chapter 3) and the bridge exists, so more species
only widen the state of the point model. That is planned for Keio.
JP: 論文に照合できる数値があるのが1・2種だから。5種は較正済みで、ブリッジもある。拡張は慶應で。

**3. Is the coupling one-way? Why?**
EN: Yes. In Klempt et al. 2024 no material parameter depends on the species, so the
composition has nothing to act on. I computed the first two-way steps in Python to
see which ones matter.
JP: 一方向。Klempt 2024では種に依存する材料定数がないため。双方向の最初のステップはPythonで試算済み。

## B. Two-way coupling (new since the deck)

**4. What did the two-way steps show?**
EN: Three steps, in Python.
- Local nutrient in the point model: in case 6 the share φ1/(φ1+φ2) varies across
  the seed from 0.03 to 0.44 (Thiele number 4). Converged in grid and time step.
- Composition into the spreading: front rate weighted by the shares, r1/r2 = 2
  from η1 = 1, η2 = 2. The mean φ at T* = 1 changes from 0.704 to 0.735 (case 3)
  and 0.648 (case 6).
- Composition into the stiffness: below 0.2 % in the seed, because the soft void
  sets the stress.
JP: 局所栄養で組成がシード内で変化（0.03〜0.44）。組成→広がりで平均φが0.704→0.735/0.648。組成→剛性は0.2%未満（周りが柔らかいので）。

**5. Where does d = 1/3 come from?**
EN: Cases 3 and 6 of Klempt et al. 2026 have η1 = 1, η2 = 2. If the front rate of a
species scales with 1/ηi, r1/r2 = 2, so d = 1/3. This scaling is my assumption;
d = 0.5 is the sensitivity case (0.750 / 0.618).
JP: η1=1, η2=2から、前線速度∝1/ηと仮定してr1/r2=2、d=1/3。仮定であることを明言。d=0.5は感度。

**6. Is there a precedent for weighting the rate by composition?**
EN: Yes, your 2023 paper (Sci. Rep. 13:11839). Summing the two growth sources
R_si φi c gives the share-weighted rate. There the growth is a local reaction, here
it is the front term. Your Table 1 uses the same rate for both species, so the
species rates are not from the paper.
JP: Soleimani 2023 の S1+S2 を足すと組成重み付きの速度になる。ただし同論文は両種同じ速度（R_s=500）。

**7. Why not implement it in ANSYS now?**
EN: Step 2 needs the element's front term, which is switched off at present
(property 4). Step 1 is easiest: the bridge already passes the local nutrient; what
is missing is reading it at the Gauss point, which I check on Monday.
JP: ステップ2は前線項が動いていないと無理。ステップ1はブリッジ側は済み、Gauss点でNut1が読めるかを月曜に確認。

## C. Model and assumptions

**8. The time link s = 0.15 and the cap φcap = 0.9: are they justified?**
EN: Neither paper gives them, so I state both as assumptions with sensitivity
studies. The cap hardly matters (0.664 to 0.668 in case 3). s matters: in case 6 the
winner only emerges for s ≳ 0.15.
JP: どちらも論文になく仮定として明記、感度解析つき。capはほぼ影響なし、sは効く（case 6はs≳0.15で勝者が出る）。

**9. Why does case 3 give 0.667 instead of the paper's 0.60?**
EN: The amount is set by the field, not by the point model's own filling. Driven by
the point model's own amount, the scheme gives back the paper to 1e-5. The second
scheme (unscaled) gives 0.614, the point model's own value.
JP: 量を場が決めるため。点モデル自身の量で駆動すれば1e-5で一致。第2スキームでは0.614。

**10. Eq. 36 or Table 1 for α?**
EN: I follow the text, Eq. 36, as the partner's element does. Under Table 1 a full
seed would not grow at all. I still need to ask Felix which one the figures use.
JP: 本文のEq.36に従う（Oliverの要素も同じ）。Table 1だと満杯のシードは成長しない。Felixへの質問はこれから。

**11. Why are the stresses so small (1e-4 Pa)?**
EN: With the published k_α = 1e-3, α − 1 stays below about 1e-3 up to T* = 1, and
E = 10 Pa. Stress scales linearly with E. Table 3 of the paper looks consistent with
μ in MPa, not Pa; that is one of my questions to Felix.
JP: k_α=1e-3で成長が1e-3以下、E=10 Pa。応力はEに比例。論文のTable 3はμがMPaなら整合（Felixに質問中）。

## D. Numerics

**12. Is the 8³ mesh fine enough?**
EN: For averages, no: on 8³ the seed averages are about 1.7 times the converged
value; 16³ is within 10 %. Peaks at the seed corners do not converge (singular
corners), so I quote averages only. A 16³ ANSYS deck is prepared for Monday.
(If asked about an independent check: the same study in the open-source solver
CalculiX agrees with my Python solver to four digits.)
JP: 平均値は8³で約1.7倍過大、16³で10%以内。角のピークは特異で収束しないので平均のみ報告。16³のANSYSは月曜。（聞かれたら：CalculiXでも4桁一致）

**13. Locking at ν = 0.49?**
EN: SOLID185 with B-bar: the mean stress changes by less than 10 % between 0.49 and
0.499. Full integration would overstate the pressure several times on 8³. I confirm
on Monday that the runs really use B-bar (ETLIST).
JP: B-barならν=0.49→0.499で平均応力の変化10%未満。完全積分だと数倍過大。月曜にETLISTでB-barを確認。

**14. The spurious pressure term in the UMAT?**
EN: Documented, not corrected. It is purely spherical, so von Mises is unaffected at
a given deformation; at ν = 0.49 it is 1.3 % of the constrained pressure.
JP: 記録のみで未修正。球成分だけなのでvon Misesには効かない。ν=0.49で拘束圧力の1.3%。

**15. Why does the biofilm not spread in ANSYS?**
EN: An input-reading slip sets the orientation weight of species 1 to zero, so the
front term never acts. With the slip fixed the term is unstable or grows in
directions set by round-off where ∇c ≈ 0. How to treat that limit is a question
for the authors.
JP: 入力読み込みのミスで前線項の重みが0。直しても∇c≈0で方向が丸め誤差で決まる。扱いは著者に確認。

## E. Validation

**16. How do you validate without stress measurements?**
EN: I do not claim validation. Everything is verification against closed forms,
the published models and a second implementation. Your 2023 paper also validates
qualitatively, by colony shape. Composition and viability (membrane-intact
ratios, Heine et al.) are measured and used in the calibration; what is missing
for the mechanics is a measured stress or deformation of a growing oral biofilm.
JP: 検証（verification）のみで妥当性確認（validation）は主張しない。Soleimani 2023も定性的。組成と生存率ψ（Heine、membrane-intact ratio）は測定済みで較正に使用。力学の妥当性確認に欠けているのは応力・変形の測定値。
