     1  ! t_growth_free.dat --- traction-free growth-path verification for usermat_biofilm.f
     2  ! ANSYS Mechanical APDL.  See RUNBOOK.md for the full Windows procedure.
     3  !   Windows (IKMHIWI03, v222), from a writable working directory:   
     4  !     "%AWP_ROOT222%\ANSYS\bin\winx64\ANSYS222.exe" -b -custom .\ANSYS.exe ^
     5  !         -i t_growth_free.dat -o out_free.txt  
     6  !   Linux:  
     7  !     ansys222 -b -custom ./ansys.e -i t_growth_free.dat -o out_free.txt
     8  ! The -custom argument must point at the executable built with usermat_biofilm.f;   
     9  ! without it ANSYS runs its own stock material and the check is meaningless.
    10  !   
    11  ! WHY THIS CASE 
    12  ! ------------- 
    13  ! t_growth_constrained.dat fixes every node (F = I exactly) and checks the  
    14  ! RESISTED-growth branch: the element wants to expand, can't, and the closed
    15  ! form predicts a hydrostatic COMPRESSION.  This deck is the opposite extreme:  
    16  ! only the six rigid-body modes are removed (a minimal 3-2-1 constraint), so
    17  ! the element is free to expand under its own growth drive. 
    18  !   
    19  ! With zero traction on every face, the equilibrium state has NO elastic
    20  ! stretch at all: the element simply grows into the shape Fg already wants, 
    21  ! so F = Fg exactly, Fe = I, and stress is EXACTLY ZERO -- independent of   
    22  ! alpha and independent of eta (there is no stress to drive viscous flow
    23  ! either, so Fv stays at I throughout). This is a second, complementary 
    24  ! closed-form check: t_growth_constrained.dat would still pass even with a  
    25  ! sign error that only shows up when the element is allowed to actually 
    26  ! deform (e.g. Fg applied with the wrong sign inside Fe = F Fg^-1 could still   
    27  ! cancel out under full kinematic constraint but will NOT cancel out here). 
    28  !   
    29  ! EXPECTED (any alpha, any eta -- see reference_values.json "free_a005")
    30  !   Je       =  1.0                          (elastic volume ratio: unchanged)  
    31  !   J        =  (1+alpha)^3                  (all volume change is growth)  
    32  !   SX=SY=SZ=SXY=SYZ=SXZ = 0                  (exactly, to solver tolerance)
    33  !   
    34  ! A nonzero stress here means either Fg or Fg^-1 is wired with the wrong
    35  ! sign/transpose, or the growth branch is coupled to the elastic branch 
    36  ! incorrectly -- a bug that t_growth_constrained.dat alone cannot catch,
    37  ! since full constraint can mask sign errors that only appear once the  
    38  ! element is free to move.
