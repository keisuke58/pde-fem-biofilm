     8  ! The -custom argument must point at the executable built with usermat_biofilm.f;   
     9  ! without it ANSYS runs its own stock material and the check is meaningless.
    10  !   
    11  ! WHY THIS CASE 
    12  ! ------------- 
    13  ! The SOLID185 uniaxial benchmark that validated the USERMAT interface on   
    14  ! 2022 R2 ran with alpha = 0, so Fg = I and the growth branch was never 
    15  ! entered.  This deck exercises growth in isolation.
    16  !   
    17  ! Every node is fully fixed, so the deformation gradient is F = I exactly at
    18  ! every integration point.  With Fg = (1+alpha) I the elastic part is   
    19  ! Fe = Fg^-1, and the Cauchy stress follows in closed form -- no FE solve is
    20  ! needed to know the answer.  Expected: pure hydrostatic COMPRESSION (the   
    21  ! element wants to grow and cannot), with all three shear components exactly
    22  ! zero. 
    23  !   
    24  ! EXPECTED (alpha = 0.05, eta = 0 -> elastic; see reference_values.json)
    25  !   Je       =  0.863837599 
    26  !   SX=SY=SZ = -1.019275856E-04 
    27  !   SXY=SYZ=SXZ = 0 
    28  !   
    29  ! A nonzero shear component means the VI/VJ Voigt map is mis-wired. 
    30  ! A positive (tensile) hydrostatic stress means Fg is being applied inverted.
