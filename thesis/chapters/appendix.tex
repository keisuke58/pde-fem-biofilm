\paragraph{Oral-fluid medium.}
The shared medium was assembled in two steps. First, quantified metabolite
concentrations in unstimulated whole saliva were drawn from the literature and
converted to exchange bounds (mmol\,gDW$^{-1}$\,h$^{-1}$) assuming a biofilm density
of ${\sim}2$\,gDW\,L$^{-1}$ and a reference growth rate of $0.1$\,h$^{-1}$:
sugars (glucose, fructose, sucrose) and lactate, all twenty amino acids plus
ornithine, B-vitamins, trace metals, purines, and inorganic salts.
Concentrations were set at the blood-plasma scale (${\sim}100\times$ realistic
saliva) so that every model remains feasible; a realistic-concentration variant
produced non-specific competition (growth suppressed by more than $50\%$ in most pairs) and
was not used. Second, AGORA2-specific cofactors absent from the saliva list were
added at minimal bounds where required for feasibility (protoheme/siroheme,
menaquinones, \textit{meso}-diaminopimelate, long-chain fatty acids,
4-hydroxybenzoate, adenosylcobalamin, glutathione, polyamines, cytidine). The O$_2$
exchange of the strictly anaerobic guilds was shut off before optimisation.

\chapter{TMCMC Implementation Details}
\label{app:tmcmc}

The four-stage GPU-accelerated TMCMC procedure of Chapter~\ref{ch:heine}
(Sections~\ref{sec:heine_twophase} and~\ref{sec:heine_gpu}) is summarised in
Algorithm~\ref{alg:tmcmc}. The GPU speed-up ($170\times$ per tempering step and
$147\times$ end to end on the matched benchmark) and the wall-clock times are in
Table~\ref{tab:heine_timing}; the fit errors and log-likelihoods are in
Table~\ref{tab:heine_rmse}.

\begin{algorithm}[htbp]
\caption{Four-stage GPU-accelerated TMCMC}
\label{alg:tmcmc}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE Data $\yobs$ (viable and total composition, live fraction), $N_p$ particles, prior box $B$, GPU device
\ENSURE MAP $\hat{\btheta}$ and posterior samples of every stage
\STATE $\triangleright$ \textbf{One TMCMC run on a box $B$ with likelihood $p(\yobs\mid\btheta)$}
\STATE draw $\{\btheta_j\}$ in $B$; $\beta_0\leftarrow0$
\WHILE{$\beta_m<1$}
  \STATE find $\beta_{m+1}$ such that the coefficient of variation of the weights equals its target, by bisection
  \STATE weights $w_j\leftarrow p(\yobs\mid\btheta_j)^{\beta_{m+1}-\beta_m}$; resample $\propto w_j$
  \STATE estimate the weighted covariance $\hat{\boldsymbol\Sigma}^{(m)}$
  \FOR{$k=1$ \TO $n_{\mathrm{mut}}$}
    \STATE propose a random-walk step scaled by $\hat{\boldsymbol\Sigma}^{(m)}$ or a differential-evolution step, alternately
    \STATE reject proposals outside $B$ without solving the model
    \STATE evaluate all $N_p$ likelihoods at once via \texttt{jax.vmap} on the GPU; accept or reject (Metropolis--Hastings)
  \ENDFOR
\ENDWHILE
\STATE $\triangleright$ \textbf{Stage (i):} composition terms only, $\psi_i$ fixed to the data, full box
\STATE $\triangleright$ \textbf{Stage (ii):} as (i), box narrowed to the stage-(i) posterior mean $\pm4$ standard deviations
\STATE $\triangleright$ \textbf{Stage (iii):} full likelihood, $\psi_i$ evolved, full (widened) box; half of the particles around the stage-(ii) MAP, half from the prior
\STATE $\triangleright$ \textbf{Stage (iv):} as (iii), box narrowed to the stage-(iii) posterior mean $\pm4$ standard deviations
\STATE if more than $20\,\%$ of the posterior mass of an entry lies in the outer $5\,\%$ of the box on one side, move that bound outwards and repeat the stage
\STATE run every stage with three seeds; accept it only if criteria (a)--(e) of Section~\ref{sec:heine_gpu} hold, otherwise repeat it with more particles or mutation steps
\RETURN $\hat{\btheta}$, posterior samples
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\paragraph{Settings.}
Stage~(i) uses $N_p=2{,}000$ to $3{,}000$ particles and the final stage
$5{,}000$ to $20{,}000$ (Table~\ref{tab:heine_timing}); the gauge is $c^*=25$.
The acceptance criteria, the definition of an identified entry and the
treatment of entries with several posterior modes are given in
Section~\ref{sec:heine_gpu}.
