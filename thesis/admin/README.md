# Thesis rules and administration (LUH, Master Maschinenbau)

Notes on the examination rules for the master's thesis, copied on 10 Oct 2026
from the `luh_summer_2026` repository (`1030_Masterarbeit/`), where the source
documents (Prüfungsordnung PDFs, `_thesis_forms/`) stay. Paths such as
`_thesis_forms/...` in these notes refer to that repository. Course material,
regulation PDFs, personal data and Felix Klempt's material are not copied.

## Current facts (10 Oct 2026)

- The applicable rules are the **MPO 2024** version (in force 1 Oct 2024), see
  `regelwerk_gaps.md`.
- **The colloquium is not graded.** In the thesis module only the Masterarbeit
  is a Prüfungsleistung; the presentation is a Studienleistung (pass or fail).
  The module grade is the thesis grade, the mean of the two examiners
  (`MPO_summary.md`, section J, corrected 13 Aug 2026).
- Dates: submission 20 Oct 2026, colloquium Wed 16 Dec 2026, 13:00 (tentative).
