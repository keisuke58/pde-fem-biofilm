\begin{frame}{What the videos show}
\small
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{5pt}
\item Biofilm phase $\phi$, growth $\alpha-1$ with $\mathbf F_g=\alpha\mathbf I$, and the von Mises stress, in 3D over time.
\item Growth model of Klempt et al.\ (2024): $\dot\phi=\beta\nabla^2\phi+k_\alpha\alpha$ (plus the bound on $\phi$),
      $\dot\alpha=k_\alpha\phi$; stiffness $E(\phi^2+f)$.
\item Homeostatic-pressure law (added in this work): growth is scaled by
      $g=\min\bigl(1,\max(0,\,1-p/p_h)\bigr)$, $p=-\tfrac13\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol\sigma$.
\item Four cases:
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item the verification problem of the thesis (seed in the centre of the 2\,mm cube),
  \item the same model with the seed in a corner or on an edge,
  \item the corner seed with and without the growth law,
  \item a tooth-like collar with synthetic four-species data (Fritsch et al.\ 2025).
  \end{enumerate}
\item Only case 1 is a thesis result. Cases 2--4 are demonstrations with the same model.
      \textbf{No experimental data are used.}
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% --------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Summary}
\small
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{6pt}
\item Case 1 reproduces the thesis value of the seed stress (0.2\,\% difference) and shows it in 3D.
\item Moving the seed to a wall breaks the symmetry; the stress then concentrates where growing biofilm meets a
      fixed surface, about 20 times the centre value.
\item The homeostatic-pressure law removes this concentration, in the cube and on the tooth-like collar
      (0.68 to 0.12 times $E$).
\item Synthetic data only (Fritsch et al.\ 2025) keep the demonstration independent of the calibration.
\item Next: a real tooth or implant surface, and the calibrated parameters of Chapter 3 at Keio.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Appendix A: notation and equations}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
$\phi$ & biofilm phase (volume fraction) \\
$\alpha$ & growth, $\mathbf F_g=\alpha\mathbf I$, $\alpha(0)=1$; growth is $\alpha-1$ \\
$\beta$, $k_\alpha$ & diffusion of $\phi$, growth rate (Klempt et al.\ 2024, Eq.\ 34/36) \\
$E(\phi^2+f)$ & stiffness, $f=10^{-3}$ (void) \\
$p$, $p_h$ & pressure $-\tfrac13\operatorname{tr}\boldsymbol\sigma$, homeostatic pressure \\
$\phi_i,\psi_i,\bar\phi_i=\phi_i\psi_i$ & point model (Klempt et al.\ 2026): volume fraction, living fraction \\
$c^*$ & nutrient scale of the point model (25 in the four-species case) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\vspace{6pt}
Cases 1--3: $\dot\phi=\beta\nabla^2\phi+k_\alpha\alpha-P\,[\max(0,\phi-1)+\min(0,\phi)]$,\;
$\dot\alpha=k_\alpha\phi\,g$ ($g=1$ without the law), $P=5$.\\
Case 4: growth from the point model in the unified element of this work, scaled by the same $g$.
\end{frame}
