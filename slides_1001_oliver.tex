\begin{frame}{Correction: the cylinder stresses I reported were wrong}
\small The two layers of every cylinder deck were meshed separately and
joined with \texttt{NUMMRG}, which merges only coincident nodes --- in the
4-condition deck, just the \textbf{4 corner nodes}. The growth layer was
essentially floating. Fixed with \texttt{VGLUE} (one shared interface mesh);
the bonded mesh converges at second order (profile error
$2.7\,\% \to 0.58\,\%$). Constants now also match the TMCMC calibration
($c^*=25$, no Hill gate, $\alpha^*=0$).

\begin{frame}{Why the conditions barely differ: the model sums them away}
\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=0.6\linewidth]{assets/condition_total_phi.png}
\end{center}
\vspace{-6pt}
\begin{itemize}\footnotesize
  \item The condition reaches the stress only through $\alpha$ (same material
        constants everywhere), and $\dot\alpha = k_\alpha\,\varphi_{\mathrm{tot}}$
        with $\varphi_{\mathrm{tot}}=\sum_i\varphi_i\psi_i$ --- \textbf{only the total}
  \item CLSM compositions are fractions: every condition starts at
        $\sum_i\varphi_i=1$ and relaxes to the same total (0.9635).
        They part only in the early $\psi$ transient: $\alpha$ spread peaks at
        2.4\,\% near $t\approx10^{-2}$, 0.003\,\% by $t=50$
  \item At the simulated time ($\sim$0.5\,\%), refining the ecology step
        \textbf{reorders the conditions}, so the ranking means nothing
\end{itemize}
\vspace{2pt}
{\footnotesize For composition to matter, the model needs species-specific growth
rates, absolute biomass instead of fractions, or composition-dependent stiffness.}
\end{frame}
